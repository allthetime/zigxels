Terrain: Currently, the terrain (GroundGrid) is generated as 
many small entities with colliders.
Critique: While this works for now, if you want true 
"Noita-like" destructibility, creating an ECS entity for 
every 5x5 pixel chunk will likely become a bottleneck. 
A hybrid approach using a raw grid/bitmask for terrain 
physics alongside ECS for moving objects would be more 
performant.
